\# CampusPrint Market Research and Pricing Plan





\## Research objective



The research examined students' printing habits, their preferred level

of assistance, and whether USB document handover would suit their needs.



The findings informed the initial service design and pricing model

for CampusPrint.



\## Method



I collected responses from 55 students using a questionnaire covering

printing frequency, current printing methods, difficulties, awareness

of printing balances, support preferences, printing options, USB

handover, and service priorities.



The results describe the respondents and should not be treated as

representative of all students at the campus.



\## Findings



\### Preferred printing support



\- Self-service instructions: 15 students (27.3%).

\- Assisted printing: 16 students (29.1%).

\- Either option: 11 students (20.0%).

\- Neither option: 13 students (23.6%).



The responses showed different support preferences. CampusPrint's

initial design therefore includes both self-service guidance and

assisted-printing requests.



A preference for assistance does not establish willingness to pay

for the service.



\### Printing frequency



\- Daily: 4 students.

\- Weekly: 21 students.

\- Monthly: 18 students.

\- Rarely: 10 students.

\- Never: 2 students.



Twenty-five respondents reported printing daily or weekly. However,

frequency alone does not establish the number of pages printed,

potential order volume, or expected sales.



\### USB document handover



\- USB handover was suitable: 48 students.

\- No USB drive available: 6 students.

\- Preferred not to hand over a USB drive: 1 student.



USB handover suited most respondents, but it would not accommodate

everyone. It remains the initial assisted-printing handover method,

with alternatives to be considered after further investigation.



\## Service decisions



CampusPrint's first version will include:



\- Information about self-service and assisted printing.

\- Instructions for printing and checking campus printing funds.

\- A price calculator using pages, copies, colour, and sidedness.

\- An assisted-printing request form.

\- A protected operator page for managing requests.

\- A summary of requests and estimated revenue.



Students will bring their documents on USB for assisted printing.



The first version will exclude document uploads, online payments,

email notifications, live campus-balance access, and direct printer

control.



\## Provisional cost assumptions



All figures are in Barbados dollars (BBD). These are planning

assumptions, not verified supplier quotations or campus rates.



\### Own-printer operation



\- Paper: BBD 0.03 per sheet.

\- Black-and-white ink or toner: BBD 0.07 per printed side.

\- Colour ink or toner: BBD 0.30 per printed side.

\- Equipment allowance: BBD 0.02 per printed side.



\### Campus-printing operation



\- Black-and-white: BBD 0.20 per printed side.

\- Colour: BBD 0.75 per printed side.

\- Paper is assumed to be included in the campus printing charge.



\### Operator time



Both assisted-printing scenarios allow five minutes per order,

valued at BBD 12 per hour.



Estimated operator-time cost per order:

5 / 60 × BBD 12 = BBD 1.00.



This fixed allowance must be reviewed for larger or more complicated

orders.



\## Proposed customer prices



For either assisted-printing source:



\- Black-and-white: BBD 0.25 per printed side.

\- Colour: BBD 0.90 per printed side.

\- Assistance fee: BBD 2.00 per order.



Students printing independently pay the applicable campus printing

charge without a CampusPrint assistance fee.



The proposed prices require cost verification and further research

into willingness to pay.



\## Calculation method



Printed sides = pages per copy × number of copies.



Single-sided sheets = printed sides.



Double-sided sheets = round up(pages per copy / 2) × copies.



Rounding takes place separately for each copy so that an odd final

page is accounted for correctly.



Estimated assisted-order revenue =

printed sides × selling price per side + assistance fee.



Own-printer modelled cost =

paper cost + ink or toner cost + equipment allowance + operator time.



Assisted campus-printing modelled cost =

campus printing charge + operator time.



\## Worked pricing example



Order: 10 black-and-white pages, 2 copies, double-sided.



\- Printed sides: 20.

\- Paper sheets: 10.

\- Estimated assisted-order revenue: BBD 7.00.



Using the service's own printer:

\- Paper: BBD 0.30.

\- Ink or toner: BBD 1.40.

\- Equipment allowance: BBD 0.40.

\- Operator time: BBD 1.00.

\- Total modelled cost: BBD 3.10.

\- Revenue less modelled costs: BBD 3.90.



Using campus printing:

\- Campus printing charge: BBD 4.00.

\- Operator time: BBD 1.00.

\- Total modelled cost: BBD 5.00.

\- Revenue less modelled costs: BBD 2.00.



Independent campus printing:

\- Estimated student charge: BBD 4.00.

\- No CampusPrint assistance fee.



The amounts remaining after modelled costs are not net profit.

Rent, travel, waste, and other unmodelled costs are excluded.



\## Limitations and next steps



The survey does not establish campus-wide demand, likely sales,

or willingness to pay.
